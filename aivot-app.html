What AIVOT is

AIVOT is a voice-training app for iOS. Record yourself speaking — in any context, any length — and AIVOT gives you objective, repeatable data on the six things that define how you come across:

  • Pitch variation
  • Pacing
  • Articulation
  • Pauses
  • Loudness
  • Tonal balance

No vague impressions. Hard numbers, every session. Then a weekly practice plan adapted to where you've improved, where you've plateaued, and which metrics matter most for your goals.

How it works

Separate tracks for every part of your life

Your voice at work isn't the same goal as your voice on a date. Set up tracks for the situations that matter — leading a team, public speaking, interviews, dating, your partner — each with its own progress, daily session, and coaching.

A weekly plan that adapts

Every week AIVOT generates a daily practice plan sized to your schedule — 2, 5, or 10 minutes — and focuses on the metrics where you have the most room to grow.

An AI coach you can talk to

Ask the coach anything about your results. Why does high pitch variability matter for leadership presence? What's a quick drill for trailing off at the end of sentences? It explains, suggests, and answers follow-up questions — grounded in what's actually measurable about your voice.

Analyze any recording

Upload a file or paste a direct audio/video link. AIVOT analyzes it with the same precision as a live session — useful for reviewing presentations, interviews, or calls after the fact.

Compare to a role model

Upload a voice you want to move toward — a speaker you admire, a manager whose delivery commands attention. AIVOT maps the gap between their metrics and yours, and tracks how you close it over time.

Honest about what it measures

AIVOT coaches observable speech delivery — things a microphone can capture and a signal can quantify. It does not measure emotions, personality, or psychological states. Guidance is educational, not medical or therapeutic. AIVOT is not a substitute for professional coaching, speech therapy, or medical advice.

Pricing

Free to start, with monthly limits on analyses and AI coach chat. AIVOT Premium unlocks unlimited analyses, full progress history, and role-model comparison, on monthly or annual plans. Cancel any time in your Apple subscription settings.
