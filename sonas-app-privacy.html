Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026  ·  Terms of Use

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Sonas Pro mobile application ("Sonas Pro", "the app") collects, how we collect it, every way we use it, who we share it with, and the controls you have. Sonas Pro is published by AIVO Development Limited ("we", "us"). Sonas Pro is a voice-training app: it records or accepts audio you provide, measures your speech delivery, and coaches you using artificial intelligence (AI) from third-party AI providers.

In short: to coach you, some of your data, including your voice recordings, is sent to our third-party AI providers. The app explains this and asks your permission before anything is sent, and you can withdraw that permission at any time in Settings → AI data sharing. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

1. What we collect

  • Voice recordings (audio data): recordings you make in the app (including your spoken replies in role-play and drills), files you upload, media you share into the app through the share extension, and media at links you paste (we fetch the media from that link). For video, we use only the audio track. Role-model recordings you add are treated the same way.
  • Transcripts and text you write: text transcripts of your recordings, your coach chat messages, messages you type for us to rephrase, and role-play scenarios you describe.
  • Account information: your email address and the account identifier of your Sign in with Apple or email/password account.
  • Profile information: the name you ask us to call you, your gender and age range, the areas you want to work on, the challenges you pick, your daily practice time, and your reminder preferences.
  • Derived metrics and coaching: scores we compute from your audio (pitch, pacing, articulation, pauses, loudness, tonal balance, words per minute, filler words), your progress over time, and the AI-generated feedback, plans and replies we create for you.
  • Usage data: counts of analyses, chat messages and role-play sessions (used to apply your plan’s monthly limits), and in-app events such as which onboarding screen you reached or which features you used (used to improve the app).
  • Subscription information: your plan (weekly, monthly or annual), trial status and expiry, verified with Apple.

We do not collect device contacts, photos (beyond a video you choose to share), location, advertising identifiers, or the contents of other apps. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we do not show third-party advertising.

2. How we collect it

  • From you: when you create an account, answer the onboarding questions, record, upload, share or link audio or video, type a message, or chat with the coach.
  • Automatically, as you use the app: usage counts and in-app events, sent from the app to our servers.
  • From Apple: your sign-in identity (if you use Sign in with Apple) and your subscription status.

3. How we use it

  • To analyse your voice and show you metrics, charts, progress, and role-model comparisons.
  • To transcribe your speech and generate AI coaching: per-recording feedback, practice plans and daily sessions, drill notes, coach chat replies, message rephrasing, role-play conversations and debriefs, and Vox’s spoken voice (see section 4).
  • To create and secure your account and send you account emails, such as your sign-up confirmation code.
  • To verify and apply your subscription.
  • To apply monthly usage limits and keep the service reliable.
  • To understand which parts of the app work and improve them (in-app events and subscription analytics).
  • To send the daily practice reminders you turn on. These are scheduled on your device.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

4. AI processing by third-party AI providers

Sonas Pro’s coaching is powered by third-party AI providers: companies that run the AI models we use for speech transcription, coaching and Vox’s voice. The providers we currently use are listed in section 5. Before any of your data is sent to them, the app shows you what is sent and why, and asks your permission. If you don’t give it, nothing is sent to our AI providers, but recording analysis, coach chat, role-play and drills can’t work. You can give or withdraw permission at any time in Settings → AI data sharing.

What is sent to our AI providers:

  • Your voice recordings (as described in section 1), so they can be transcribed into text.
  • Transcripts, coach chat messages, messages you ask us to rephrase, and role-play scenarios and replies.
  • Your voice scores and the goals and challenges you picked, so the feedback fits you.
  • The text of Vox’s replies, so it can be spoken aloud.

What is never sent to our AI providers: your name, email address, age range, gender, or account identifier. (A recording or message will of course contain anything you choose to say or write in it.)

How our AI providers handle it: they process this data on our behalf, only to provide Sonas Pro to you. We only use AI providers whose terms say that data we send them is not used to train their models and is retained for no more than 30 days (for abuse monitoring) before deletion. Each is contractually bound to protect your data to a standard at least equal to this policy.

5. Who we share data with

We share data only with these service providers, only to run Sonas Pro:

  • AI providers: speech transcription, AI coaching and text-to-speech, as described in section 4. Currently: OpenAI. If we add or change an AI provider, we will update this list and the app will ask for your permission again.
  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and file storage. Your recordings and data are stored in our private Supabase project, protected by row-level security so only the Sonas Pro backend can read them.
  • Railway: hosts the Sonas Pro backend servers that process your requests.
  • Resend: delivers account emails, such as sign-up confirmation codes, to your email address.
  • RevenueCat: subscription analytics. Receives your account identifier, your subscription purchases and renewals, and the areas, challenges and age range you chose during onboarding, so we can understand subscriptions by group. It does not receive your recordings, transcripts or messages.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases, and subscription verification.

Each provider processes your data only on our instructions and under written terms, including data-processing terms, that require it to protect your data with the same or equal protection as described in this policy. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

6. Where data lives, and for how long

  • Voice recordings: stored in our Supabase storage and deleted automatically 30 days after upload. Computed metrics and progress are kept while your account is active so your charts keep working.
  • Data sent to our AI providers: retained by them for no more than 30 days, as described in section 4.
  • Account, profile, transcripts, metrics, chat messages: kept while your account exists, and deleted when you delete your account.
  • Subscription records: kept for as long as needed to honour the subscription and to meet Apple and tax-record requirements.

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Your rights and controls

  • AI data sharing: give or withdraw permission to send data to our AI providers at any time in Settings → AI data sharing.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your recordings, progress, role models, profile, and chat from our database.
  • Sign out: Settings → Sign Out.
  • Access or export: contact us at hello@aivodevelopment.com and we will provide a copy of the data on your account.
  • Microphone permission: revoke it at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → Sonas Pro.
  • If you are in the UK, EEA or a similar jurisdiction, you also have rights under the GDPR (or equivalent law), including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, data portability, withdrawing consent, and lodging a complaint with your supervisory authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office). Our legal bases are: performance of our contract with you (to provide the app), your consent (for processing by our AI providers), and our legitimate interests (to secure and improve the service).

8. Children

Sonas Pro is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Traffic between the app, our servers and our providers is encrypted with HTTPS. Database access is protected by row-level security; only the Sonas Pro backend can read user data. Subscription purchases are cryptographically verified against Apple’s certificates on our servers.

10. Changes

If we materially change this policy we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. If we change what is sent to our AI providers, or add or change an AI provider, the app will ask for your permission again.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: hello@aivodevelopment.com
