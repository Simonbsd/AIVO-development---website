Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026  ·  Terms of Use

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Sonas mobile application ("Sonas", "the app") collects when you use it, how we use that information, and the controls you have. Sonas is published by AIVO Development Limited ("we", "us"). Sonas is a voice-training app that records or accepts audio you provide, computes objective speech-delivery metrics, and provides coaching based on those metrics.

1. What we collect

  • Voice recordings (audio data) — recordings you make in the app, files you upload, or media you share into the app via the share extension. For video shares we extract only the audio track on your device before upload.
  • Account information — the email address and identifier associated with your Sign in with Apple or email/password account.
  • Profile information — gender, age range, and the goals you select during onboarding, used to tailor your plan.
  • Derived metrics — numeric scores we compute from your audio (pitch, pacing, articulation, pauses, loudness, tonal balance) and the AI coaching feedback we generate from them.
  • Usage data — counts of analyses and chat messages, used to enforce free-tier limits.
  • Subscription entitlement — your active product (monthly/annual) and expiry, verified server-side against Apple.

We do not collect device contacts, photos, location, advertising identifiers, or the contents of other apps. We do not use tracking technologies or third-party advertising.

2. How we use it

  • To analyse your voice and produce metrics, charts, role-model comparisons, and AI coaching for you.
  • To authenticate you and manage your account.
  • To verify and apply your subscription entitlement.
  • To meter and rate-limit AI usage so the service stays reliable.

3. Third-party processors

We use a small set of vetted providers strictly to operate the service:

  • Supabase — database and audio storage. Your audio and metadata sit in our private Supabase project, protected by row-level security so only the Sonas backend can read it.
  • OpenAI — large language model. When we generate your coaching plan, per-recording feedback, or coach-chat replies we send derived numeric metrics and chat text to OpenAI's API. We do not send your raw audio. Per OpenAI's API terms, API content is not used to train their models.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases, and StoreKit transaction verification.

4. Where data lives, and for how long

  • Voice recordings — stored in our Supabase storage and deleted automatically 30 days after upload. Computed metrics and progress snapshots are retained while your account is active so your charts continue to work.
  • Account, profile, metrics, chat messages — retained while your account exists. Deleted when you delete your account.
  • Subscription receipts / entitlements — retained for the period required to honour the subscription and to comply with Apple and tax-record requirements.

5. Your rights and controls

  • Delete your account — Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your recordings, progress, role models, profile, and chat from our database.
  • Sign out — Settings → Sign Out.
  • Access or export — contact us at hello@aivodevelopment.com and we will provide a copy of the data on your account.
  • Microphone permission — revoke at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy → Microphone → Sonas.
  • If you are in the EEA, UK, or similar jurisdictions, you also have rights under GDPR (or equivalent) including access, correction, restriction, and lodging a complaint with your supervisory authority.

6. Children

Sonas is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Database access is protected by row-level security; only the Sonas backend can read user data. Subscription entitlements are cryptographically verified against Apple's roots server-side.

8. Changes

If we materially change this policy we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: hello@aivodevelopment.com
