Account & subscription

How do I cancel my AIVOT Premium subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. To cancel:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name at the top → Subscriptions.
  3. Tap AIVOT → Cancel Subscription.

You'll keep access until the end of the current billing period. You can also go directly to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Sign in to AIVOT with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe, open Settings → Subscription in the app, and tap Restore Purchases. Your Premium entitlement will reactivate. If it doesn't, email us and we'll fix it manually.

How do I delete my account?

Inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your recordings, progress, role models, profile, and chat history from our database. The action can't be undone.

If you can't reach the in-app option for any reason, email us from the address on your account and we'll process the deletion manually within one business day.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds for App Store subscriptions are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the AIVOT purchase, and choose Request a refund. We don't have the ability to refund App Store purchases directly, but if Apple declines and you think it was unfair, email us and we'll do what we can to help.

I forgot my password / can't sign in.

If you signed up with email, tap Forgot password on the sign-in screen and we'll send a reset link. If you signed up with Sign in with Apple, manage that on your iPhone in Settings → Apple ID → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple → AIVOT.

Using AIVOT

My recording failed or won't analyze. What now?

First, check the basics:

  • Microphone permission is on: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → AIVOT.
  • You have an active internet connection — analysis runs on our servers.
  • The recording is longer than a few seconds (very short clips can't be analyzed).

If it still fails, try closing and reopening the app. If the problem persists, email us with the device model, iOS version, and roughly when it happened so we can find it in our logs.

Can I upload a file or a YouTube link?

You can upload local audio or video files (the app extracts the audio on your device). You can also paste a direct media link — for example a .mp3 or .m4a URL.

Streaming-platform links (YouTube, TikTok, Vimeo, Instagram) are not supported, because downloading from those platforms would violate their terms of service. To analyze a video from one of those platforms, download the file through their official export tools first and upload it.

Why are my voice metrics different from what I expected?

AIVOT measures six observable signals: pitch variation, pacing, articulation, pauses, loudness, and tonal balance. They are not the same as how you feel you sound. A confident speech can score "low pitch variation" if your pitch sits in a narrow band, even if your delivery felt expressive in the moment.

The numbers are the input to the practice plan, not a verdict. Tap the ⓘ next to any metric in the app for a plain-English explanation of what it means.

What's the difference between "tracks"?

Tracks are separate practice contexts. Your voice for leading at work isn't the same goal as your voice on a date. Each track keeps its own recordings, progress, and AI coaching, so improving your pacing at work doesn't muddy your "warmth at home" baseline.

You can have up to two tracks active at a time. Add, pause, or remove tracks in Settings → Tracks.

I ran out of free AI coach messages. What happens?

Free accounts get a monthly allowance of AI coach chat messages. When you reach the limit, the coach will tell you and offer Premium. Your existing chat history is kept — you can read it any time. The quota resets at the start of each calendar month.

Data & privacy

How do I get a copy of my data?

Email us at hello@aivodevelopment.com from the address on your account and we'll send a copy of your account data within one business day. This includes your profile, recordings metadata, computed metrics, and chat messages.

Do you train AI models on my voice or chat messages?

No. We do not use your recordings or your chat messages to train any AI models, and we do not sell or share them with third parties. Voice recordings are stored on our private Supabase backend and deleted after a short retention window. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.
